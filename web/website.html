    Our story

    35 Years Behind the Wheel

    Divya Travels began with one driver, one car, and a simple promise — get every passenger to their destination safely, on time, every time. Over 35 years, that promise never changed, even as the roads, the cars, and the city around us did.

    What started as everyday city rides slowly grew into full-day temple tour packages, taking families to Kancheepuram, Mamallapuram, and beyond — planned personally, driven personally, and priced fairly.

    Today, Divya Travels is trusted by thousands of families across Chennai for both quick city cabs and long temple pilgrimages, all backed by decades of real driving experience and genuine care for every passenger.
